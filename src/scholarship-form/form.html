Vicky Jacoby Scholarship

Application Instructions
Deadline
April 15th of the current school year
Email completed applications to
Karen Gregory
lkgleander363@gmail.com
  1. This scholarship is being awarded by Leander Chapter #73, Order of the Eastern Star.
  2. A minimum of 3 scholarships can be awarded in the amount of $500.00.
  3. All applicants must be graduating seniors, who plan on attending a College, University or trade school to further their education and can provide proof of registration or enrollment in said institution.
  4. Recipients will be chosen based on points awarded for academic achievement and school/community involvement.
  5. Point values are listed on the application.
  6. Each application must also have a brief essay attached (50-100 words) describing how this scholarship money would help further the applicant's plans for the future.
  7. Completed applications must be received no later than April 15th of the current school year, to: Karen Gregory, Leander Chapter #73 OES, emailed to lkgleander363@gmail.com.
  8. Chosen recipients will be notified by phone or email. We would like recipients and their families to join us for dinner and an awards ceremony at our chapter meeting on the third Tuesday in May at 6:30pm, held in the historic Norton Moses Masonic Lodge at 201 Sonny Dr in Leander.
